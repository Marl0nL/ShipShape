A Ship is a VM where your agents work. FleetLedger makes it, waits until it is provisioned and checked, and then you sign it in to Claude (and GitHub and Google Cloud if you use them).

It starts once you sign the Ship in to Claude (the next step), and on every boot. With Remote Control on, it shows in the Claude app (phone, desktop or claude.ai) under your account, so you can talk to it from anywhere. Add more later on the Ship's page.
